What is Spectacle

A modern finance function, built on all your data

Your accountant keeps the books. Spectacle connects them with the rest of the business: bank, invoicing, sales and customer systems, payroll, contracts and operational data. The result is a finance function that does what it always has, only faster, more thoroughly and with an eye on what comes next.

  • Your whole business in one picture We go beyond the accounts and draw on every source that shows how the company is really doing. You see the connections, not just the totals.
  • The fundamentals, done properly Financial statements, budgets, cash, forecasts and board reporting. The finance function every company needs, delivered accurately and on time.
  • Built for today's pace Our own tools and methods make heavy analysis fast. What used to take weeks takes days, and you get answers while the question still matters.

How it works

From first meeting to steady delivery

  1. Discovery meeting

    A short online meeting about your company, your data and what you need. We do our homework in public sources beforehand, and never ask for anything we can find ourselves.

  2. Set-up

    We gather your accounts and the other data your business holds, and bring them together: financial statements from your first year to the last closed year, and a financial model with budgets. It gives you and us the same picture from month one. The set-up is included in the monthly fee.

  3. Ongoing delivery

    Reports, forecasts and follow-up that stay current as new data comes in. A fixed monthly fee for what your company actually needs.

Safe and straightforward

The principles we work by

  • An addition to your accountant No change of systems or accountant. Spectacle draws on the systems you already use, as an addition, not a replacement.
  • Your data stays yours Data is treated as confidential and used only for the work we do for you. The processing is governed by a separate data processing agreement.
  • A person is accountable Our tools do the heavy lifting, but every delivery is reviewed and approved by your adviser before it reaches you. Spectacle is accountable for the quality.
  • Predictable economics A fixed monthly fee for the ongoing delivery. Work beyond the agreed scope is always cleared with you before it is done.
